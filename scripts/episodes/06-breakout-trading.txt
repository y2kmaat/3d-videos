When price breaks out, it can move fast. Here's how breakout traders catch it. Find a level the price has hit several times, like a ceiling. Wait for a candle to close above it, not just poke through. Then check the volume. A real breakout usually comes with a spike in volume. Enter on the close, or on a retest of the level, with your stop just below it. Fake breakouts happen, so always use a stop. This is education, not financial advice.
