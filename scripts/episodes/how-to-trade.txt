Most people lose money trading because they skip the boring part. Here's how to actually start. First, pick one market and learn it. Not ten, one. Second, never risk more than one percent of your account on a single trade. Third, set your stop loss before you enter, not after. Fourth, write down every trade in a journal, the wins and the losses. And fifth, practice on a demo account until you're profitable for three months straight. Trading is a skill, not a lottery ticket. This is education, not financial advice.
