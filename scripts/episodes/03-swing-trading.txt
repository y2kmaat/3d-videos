Swing trading is made for people with a day job. Here's why. You hold trades for a few days to a few weeks, catching one swing in the price. You use daily and four hour charts, so you only check them once or twice a day. Look for a pullback in a trend, and enter when price bounces. Put your stop below the last swing low. Fewer trades, less screen time, more patience. This is education, not financial advice.
