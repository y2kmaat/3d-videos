Day traders never hold a position overnight. Here's the playbook. Every trade opens and closes the same day, so overnight news can't hurt you. Most day traders focus on the first two hours after the market opens, when volume is highest. They plan the entry, the exit, and the stop loss before the bell. And they set a daily loss limit. Hit it, and you're done for the day. Day trading takes time, focus, and capital. This is education, not financial advice.
