Scalpers make dozens of trades a day for tiny profits. Here's how scalping works. You trade on one minute or five minute charts and hold for seconds to minutes. You aim for small moves, a few ticks or pips at a time. Because the targets are small, costs matter, so you need tight spreads and low fees. And you need strict discipline, because one bad trade can wipe out twenty good ones. Scalping is fast, intense, and not for beginners. This is education, not financial advice.
