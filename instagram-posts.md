## 01 · Scalping
Trading for SECONDS. ⚡️ Scalping = dozens of trades a day for tiny profits.

→ 1–5 minute charts, hold seconds to minutes
→ Small moves add up, so tight spreads + low fees matter
→ One bad trade can wipe out twenty good ones

Fast, intense, and not for beginners. Save this before you try it. 📌

Education, not financial advice.
`#scalping #scalpingstrategy`

## 02 · Day trading
No trades overnight. 🌙 That's the #1 rule of day trading.

→ In and out the same day, so overnight news can't hurt you
→ Most of the action is in the first 2 hours after the open
→ Plan entry, exit and stop loss BEFORE the bell
→ Set a daily loss limit. Hit it = done for the day

Save this for your next session. 📌

Education, not financial advice.
`#daytrading #daytradingtips`

## 03 · Swing trading
Got a 9-to-5? This is the trading style built for you. 💼

→ Hold for days to weeks and catch one swing
→ Daily + 4H charts: check once or twice a day
→ Buy the pullback in a trend, enter on the bounce
→ Stop goes below the last swing low

Less screen time, more patience. 📌

Education, not financial advice.
`#swingtrading #swingtrader`

## 04 · Position trading
Hold for months, sometimes years. 🗓️

→ Start with the big picture: weekly + monthly charts and fundamentals
→ Ride the long-term trend, ignore the daily noise
→ Wider stops, smaller position sizes
→ Closest thing to investing, but with an exit plan

Patience IS the strategy. 📌

Education, not financial advice.
`#positiontrading #longtermtrading`

## 05 · Trend following
The trend is your friend… until it ends. 📈

→ Higher highs + higher lows = uptrend
→ Lower highs + lower lows = downtrend
→ Only trade in that direction
→ Use the 50-day moving average as your guide
→ Get out when the structure breaks

Save this. 📌

Education, not financial advice.
`#trendfollowing #trendtrading #movingaverage`

## 06 · Breakout trading
When price breaks out, it can move FAST. 🚀

→ Find a level price has hit several times
→ Wait for a candle to CLOSE above it
→ Check for a volume spike
→ Enter on the close or the retest, stop just below the level

Fake breakouts happen. Always use a stop. 📌

Education, not financial advice.
`#breakouttrading #priceaction`

## 07 · Range trading
Markets spend a lot of time going sideways. Here's how to trade it. ↔️

→ Support = the floor price keeps bouncing off
→ Resistance = the ceiling it keeps getting rejected from
→ Buy near support, sell near resistance
→ Stop just outside the range, because when it breaks, it can break hard

Best in calm, quiet markets. 📌

Education, not financial advice.
`#rangetrading #supportandresistance`

## 08 · Moving average crossover
Two lines. One signal. ✖️

→ Fast average (20-day) vs slow average (50-day)
→ Fast crosses ABOVE slow = momentum turning up
→ Fast crosses BELOW slow = momentum turning down
→ It lags, and gives false signals in sideways markets

Use it WITH a trend. 📌

Education, not financial advice.
`#movingaverage #technicalanalysis`

## 09 · RSI
Is the move running out of steam? 🔋 The RSI can tell you.

→ Relative Strength Index, runs from 0 to 100
→ Above 70 = overbought
→ Below 30 = oversold
→ In a strong trend, RSI can stay overbought for weeks

Wait for confirmation before you trade it. 📌

Education, not financial advice.
`#rsi #technicalanalysis #indicators`

## 10 · News trading
Big news can move markets in seconds. 📰

→ Check the economic calendar every morning (rate decisions, jobs reports)
→ At the release: spreads widen and price whips both ways
→ Many traders wait ~15 minutes, then trade the direction that holds
→ Size down, because news trades are volatile

Save this before the next big release. 📌

Education, not financial advice.
`#newstrading #economiccalendar #forextrading`
